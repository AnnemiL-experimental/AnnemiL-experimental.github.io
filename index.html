Chapter 01

About

Born in Xi'an, Shaanxi. Started in philosophy, then pivoted to engineering. Earned my B.S. in Engineering, majoring in Biomedical Engineering with tons of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science coursework, at Miami University, where I worked across tissue optics, behavioral neuroscience, flexible bioelectronics, and embedded biosignal acquisition systems.

Decades of living with psychiatric illness led me toward a single question: How do we physically intervene in mental suffering? I'm now a graduate student at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, conducting thesis research at the Center for Advanced Circuit Therapeutics (CACT). My current work focuses on deep brain stimulation-evoked potentials, cortical responses, and target engagement in obsessive-compulsive disorder.

Outside the lab, I make things. Drawing, painting, calligraphy, photography, writing. I watch too many films and listen to too much music. I play video games late at night.

Clinical

DBS Evoked Potentials in OCD & Depression

Graduate research at the Center for Advanced Circuit Therapeutics (CACT), Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai: studying evoked potentials from deep brain stimulation in patients with obsessive-compulsive disorder and depression.

Panic & Dissociative State Transitions

Interactive

Before The Thought Arrives

Interactive 3D brain visualization mapping EEG frequency bands and emotional states onto independent anatomical region volumes. Each state drives cfos-style fluorescent puncta, live waveform rendering, and procedurally generated stream-of-consciousness text. Built with Three.js + React.

Automated Behavior Quantification Pipeline

Built an end-to-end behavioral neuroscience analysis pipeline integrating DeepLabCut pose estimation and SimBA-based behavior classification. The workflow converts raw top-view mouse videos into tracked body-part coordinates, annotated behavioral states, and quantitative outputs for downstream analysis.

Internal State-Dependent Modulation of Defensive Behavior in Mice

Behavioral neuroscience poster on how baseline anxiety and acute trauma shape defensive behavior in mice across elevated zero maze, fear conditioning, open field, and resident intruder assays.

Capstone

P.I.P. · Personal Insulin Pal

Senior capstone colorimetric glucose biosensor for pediatric users. I independently managed the electrical and firmware subsystem: MCP6002 transimpedance amplifier, BPW34 photodiode integration, ESP32 signal acquisition, OLED display, ambient-light correction, and kinetic calibration firmware.

ESP32-WROOM-32E · MCP6002 TIA · BPW34 · SSD1331 OLED · GOx/HRP/TMB

Environmental

Mn Removal from Potable Water

Investigated adsorption kinetics using activated biomass for low-cost water treatment. Conducted pyrolysis, BET surface-area analysis, TGA, colorimetry, and pH optimization. Team achieved >90% manganese removal efficiency.

Mechanics

Materials Testing: Silicone & Porcine Tissue

Characterized nonlinear hyperelastic and viscoelastic behavior of silicone rubber and porcine skeletal muscle under uniaxial tension and compression. Fitted four-term Ogden model (R² = 0.997).

Ogden Hyperelastic Modeling · Stress Relaxation

Mooney–Rivlin Hyperelastic Model

Derived PK1 stress predictions under uniaxial tension and simple shear for incompressible Mooney–Rivlin materials. Demonstrated nonlinear strain-stiffening in tension and linear shear response.
